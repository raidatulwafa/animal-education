📖 About the Elephant

Elephants are large mammals known for their long trunks, large ears and strong bodies. They are social animals and often live in groups. Elephants use their trunks for many activities including breathing, smelling, touching and picking up food.

🐘 Size One of the largest land animals
🌿 Food Mainly plants
👥 Social Often live in groups
👃 Trunk Used for many functions

📖 The Elephant in the Quran

The elephant is connected with the story described in Surah Al-Fil (Chapter 105). The surah tells about the people of the elephant and Allah's protection of the Ka'bah.

أَلَمْ تَرَ كَيْفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِأَصْحَابِ الْفِيلِ

"Have you not seen how your Lord dealt with the companions of the elephant?"

— Surah Al-Fil, 105:1
